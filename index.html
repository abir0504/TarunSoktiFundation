About Us

Tarun Shakti Foundation, established in 2026, is a humanitarian, non-political organization dedicated to empowering young individuals through education, skill development, and community service. Guided by the principle "For the sake of the Ummah, with the Sunnah," we strive to build a compassionate society rooted in Islamic values and sustainable development.

Our Programs

Educational Workshops

Interactive sessions focusing on Islamic knowledge, modern skills, and personal development to nurture well-rounded individuals.

Skill Training Sessions

Practical training in various trades and technologies, empowering youth with marketable skills for sustainable livelihoods.

Community Outreach

Grassroots initiatives including charity drives, health awareness campaigns, and support for underprivileged communities.

What We've Accomplished

500+ Lives Impacted

Through our educational programs, we've reached over 500 young individuals, providing them with knowledge and skills for a brighter future.

Community Projects

Successfully organized multiple charity drives and community service events, fostering unity and compassion within our Ummah.

Youth Empowerment

Trained dozens of young leaders who are now actively contributing to their communities and spreading positive change.

Our Future Plans

Expanded Educational Reach

Launch online platforms and mobile units to bring quality Islamic education to remote areas and underserved communities.

Sustainable Development Initiatives

Partner with local businesses to create vocational training centers and micro-finance programs for economic empowerment.

Global Humanitarian Network

Build international partnerships to address global challenges while staying true to our Islamic principles and values.
